# Evil Henchman Tasks

A running log of schemes for The Garden's henchmen. Each new day's batch is appended below.

## Tuesday, 2026-10-13

1. **Operation Squeaky Revolving Door** (Easy, 2 henchmen) - Oil nothing at all. Leave the lobby door of the Hero Council's headquarters squeaking loudly every time it opens, so that every visitor announces themselves to the snack bar.
2. **Operation Borrowed Blueprint** (Medium, 3 henchmen) - Slip into the city archive disguised as a tax auditor and photograph the floor plan of the hero's secret hangout, then return the original binder with a tidy index tab.
3. **Operation Lukewarm Lava** (Medium, 4 henchmen) - Keep the hollow volcano lair's lava moat at a pleasantly tepid temperature for the next fortnight, which will make the villain's bath approximately three times more smug.
4. **Operation Midnight Catering** (Easy, 2 henchmen) - Deliver the nightly snack order for the laser-guarded vault, making sure every cracker is labeled with a sinister little skull sticker and that the laser grid is kept politely switched off during the delivery.
5. **Operation Forged Parking Ticket** (Medium, 3 henchmen) - Issue a convincing (and completely fake) parking citation to the hero's getaway van, then ensure the van is towed to the lair's basement garage for "impoundment."
6. **Operation Grumble Club** (Easy, 1 henchman) - Found a support group for henchmen who feel underappreciated. Meetings are held in the broom closet, snacks are provided, and the only rule is that everyone must complain loudly and with enthusiasm.
7. **Operation Missing Monument** (Deadly, 6 henchmen) - Relocate the city's bronze statue of a beloved hero to the middle of the Garden's private golf course, where it can stand as a dramatic reminder of who is really in charge of the skyline.
8. **Operation Overly Complicated Umbrella** (Wildcard, 2 henchmen) - Build a seventeen-part mechanical umbrella that opens only when the rain stops, and deploy it over the mayor during the next outdoor press conference, just to see whether anyone ever asks why it is inside out.

## Tuesday, 2026-10-13 (Second Batch)

1. **Operation Alphabetized Doom** (Easy, 2 henchmen) - Reorganize the lair's supply closet alphabetically by each villain's middle name, then label every shelf with sinister Latin, such as "Paperclips (Ominous)."
2. **Operation Cardigan Infiltration** (Medium, 2 henchmen) - Attend the hero's charity bake sale in a cardigan and a fake mustache, and quietly replace every cupcake with one carrying an extra-large sprinkle of doubt.
3. **Operation Cuckoo of Dread** (Easy, 1 henchman) - Swap the Hero Council's wall clock for a cuckoo clock that shouts "Muahaha!" at the top of every hour, then deny all knowledge of the bird's previous career.
4. **Operation Gloomy Commute Playlist** (Easy, 1 henchman) - Compile a morning playlist of ominous organ music for the henchman carpool, with a strict rule that every song must end on a dramatic chord.
5. **Operation Velvet Rope Velvet Rope** (Medium, 3 henchmen) - Work the door at the Garden's exclusive Doom Lounge opening night, admitting only guests who can deliver a convincing scowl on demand.
6. **Operation Overinflated Parade** (Medium, 4 henchmen) - Fill the hero's parade route with three hundred helium balloons shaped like a frowning moon, then tie every one of them to the nearest lamppost with extremely thorough knots.
7. **Operation Seating Chart Sabotage** (Deadly, 5 henchmen) - Infiltrate the Hero Council's annual banquet and rearrange every place card so the rivals are seated together and the mayor is placed directly beside the sternest pickle on the buffet.
8. **Operation Pet Rock Congress** (Wildcard, 2 henchmen) - Convene a formal parliament of the lair's pet rocks, complete with minutes, a seating chart, and a sternly worded vote on whether the lair needs a second moat.
